# Truth as a Practice

*A topic in the Hacefiance constellation — July 2026. Published on Substack (FR/EN). CC0 license.*

---

## The Greek question

Since the Greeks, truth is not a thing one possesses. *Alētheia* means unveiling — a gesture, not an object. Socrates does not know; he knows that he does not know. Truth is a direction, not a destination. Unreachable by construction, yet oriented.

We sense it through a feeling of concordance, when intuition and reflection cease to contradict each other. This is not a proof. It is a compass.

And the movement is fractal. We think we hold an answer; it opens onto a finer structure that poses the same question at its own scale. The work reveals itself at every step without ever closing.

## An interface property

Truth-as-practice has the same status as time, meaning, and the compass in the Hacefiance framework:

- It does not exist at the level of the whole — the whole has nothing to seek, it *is*.
- It exists only for a part reducing its gap with the rest.
- It is an interface property, not a fundamental one.

The gesture that orients itself toward the real is truth. The object found is only a momentary trace of it.

## Finished form / movement of structuring

All inquiry oscillates between two poles.

The finished final form: a perfect crystal, a closed truth, a dogma, an index that claims to measure life. It is beautiful and dead. It has ceased to dissipate, to seek, to live. In the language of the framework: thermodynamic equilibrium. The maximal entropy of meaning.

The movement of structuring: a thought that stays open, that maintains the gap, that keeps channeling. It is alive precisely because it does not conclude. It is a dissipative structure: it only stands by remaining out of equilibrium.

A philosophy of complexification cannot conclude without betraying itself. To conclude is to come to a halt. The central Hacefiance text ends on questions — not out of elegance, but out of physical necessity.

## The temptation to measure

Assembly theory (Cronin & Walker, 2023) claims to quantify selection and evolution through an index. It is contested for reducing to already-known Shannon entropy, and for overselling — "unifying physics and biology, redefining time."

This is a mirror warning. Hacefiance is exposed to the same temptation: taking a true intuition — complexity accumulates through selection — and claiming that it measures everything. The line not to cross is clear: describe a slope, yes; claim to have measured it, no. The insistence on "what this does not prove" is the only protection against that shipwreck.

## What does not move

There remains a question the framework cannot close, and must not close.

Everything complexifies, everything emerges, everything stays open. But logic — the grammar without which no structuring would be possible — is it also in movement? Or is it the immobile condition of all the rest?

I do not know. That is the only honest way to end a text on truth as a practice: to stop where one no longer knows.
